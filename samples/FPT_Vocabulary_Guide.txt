Unit 1: English Entry Assessment Preparation - Tenses and Structures

1. Present Perfect Tense (Thì hiện tại hoàn thành)
- Form: S + have/has + V3/V-ed
- Usage: Used to describe actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past or actions that started in the past and continue to the present.
- Example: "She has lived in Da Nang for five years."
- Common signal words: already, yet, never, ever, just, since, for.

2. Conditional Sentences Type 2 (Câu điều kiện loại 2)
- Form: If + S + V2/V-ed (were for all subjects), S + would/could + V-inf
- Usage: Expresses hypothetical, unreal situations in the present or future.
- Example: "If I had more free time, I would study another foreign language."

3. Relative Clauses (Mệnh đề quan hệ)
- Who: used for people as subject ("The student who scored highest received a scholarship.")
- Which: used for things and animals ("The book which is on the table belongs to the library.")
- Whose: indicates possession ("The candidate whose application was approved will start next Monday.")

4. Passive Voice in Academic Reading (Câu bị động)
- Form: S + be + V3/V-ed (+ by O)
- Example: "The entrance examination is conducted twice every semester by FPT University."
